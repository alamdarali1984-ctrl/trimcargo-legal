Privacy Policy for TrimCargo

Effective date: 4 August 2026 · Operator: Syed Ali · Country: Australia · Contact: alamdar_ali@outlook.com

TrimCargo ("the Apps") is a suite of Android, iOS and Windows draft survey, hold trimming and loading-limit assistant for marine cargo officers. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what data the App handles, where it is stored, and what control you have over it.

1. Summary in plain language

  • Your vessel, voyage, survey and calculation data is stored locally by default.
  • Android, iOS and Windows offer an optional TrimCargo Cloud account. Nothing is uploaded automatically: only a vessel and voyage you explicitly choose to upload is sent to the cloud.
  • Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase is contacted only to offer, confirm and restore the optional Pro unlock on that store platform.
  • The App does not contain analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs that phone home.
  • A cloud account is optional. Local survey work, manual archives and reports do not require sign-in.
  • TrimCargo source code, camera photos and reports are not uploaded to TrimCargo Cloud.

2. What data the App collects

The App collects only the data you enter or capture in order to perform draft survey calculations. It stays on your device unless you sign in and explicitly upload a selected vessel and voyage to TrimCargo Cloud.

  • Vessel information you type in (ship name, IMO, dimensions, hydrostatic tables, hatch data).
  • Survey readings (drafts, densities, ballast, fuel, etc.) you enter or capture.
  • Photos of draft marks you capture with the camera or pick from your gallery, used only for visual reference and on-device draft reading.
  • Voyage records you create (initial / running / trimming / final surveys, calculations, results).
  • App preferences (theme, units, biometric lock setting, archive folder location).
  • Access status (trial start/end, reviewer-access expiry, and whether Google Play or Apple has confirmed the Pro unlock).
  • Optional cloud account data (email address, Supabase user identifier, registered-device identifier, platform, device display name, app version and last-seen time).
  • Optional cloud workspace data (the selected vessel Master CSV, selected voyage archive JSON, revision number, update time and source-device identifier).

3. Where data is stored

All data is stored locally on your device, inside the App's private storage area (for example Android Room/DataStore, the iOS application container, or the Windows user profile). The operating system isolates this area from other apps. Data leaves your device only when you choose an export/share action or explicitly upload a vessel and voyage from Cloud Workspace.

Optional cloud account and workspace records are hosted for TrimCargo by Supabase in its Oceania (Sydney, Australia) region. Supabase processes those records as our service provider so the signed-in user can retrieve the same workspace on another supported TrimCargo device. Row-level access rules restrict each record to its owning signed-in account.

4. Permissions used and why

  • Camera — only when you tap a camera-related action in the App, used to photograph draft marks for on-device measurement and visual reference. Photos are saved on the device only.
  • Photo / video picker — opens the standard Android Photo Picker so you can optionally re-use an image or video you already have. The App receives only the file you pick; it has no access to your wider gallery.
  • Biometric — optional. If you enable it in Settings, the App uses your device's fingerprint or face unlock to authorise sensitive ship operations. The biometric data itself never leaves the secure hardware of your device and is not accessible to the App.
  • Storage / document picker — only when you choose to export or import a voyage archive, vessel Master CSV, or report. The App reads or writes the single file you choose; it does not scan or browse your storage.

The App does not request location, contacts, microphone, calendar, call logs, SMS, or any other sensitive permission.

5. On-device libraries

The App embeds these on-device libraries for calculations and image processing:

  • OpenCV — image processing for draft-mark detection. Runs entirely on the device. No network calls.
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition (on-device) — reads digits from draft-mark photos. As per Google's ML Kit terms, the on-device API does not send images or text to Google.
  • AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, CameraX, Media3 — standard Android UI / camera / video components.
  • Google Play Billing — connects to Google Play only when checking product availability, purchasing, acknowledging, or restoring the optional Pro unlock. The App never receives your card or payment-account details.

6. Manual voyage and vessel transfer

TrimCargo can export a voyage as an encrypted .draftar archive at your request. The archive is saved to a folder you choose (or shared with another app you pick) and is encrypted using AES-256-GCM. The app does not transmit the archive anywhere; whatever happens to the file after export is under your control.

TrimCargo can also export vessel particulars, hydrostatic rows, and hatch limits as a readable Master CSV for import on another TrimCargo platform. Master CSV files are not encrypted. Review the destination before sharing and protect the exported file as vessel operational data. Import creates a separate local vessel and does not silently replace a vessel with the same IMO number.

7. No analytics, no advertising, no profiling

The App contains no analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Crashlytics, no Sentry, no Facebook SDK, no AdMob, etc.). It does not build a usage profile about you and does not personalise behaviour based on identifiers. The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 18 and is intended for adult marine professionals only.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal or operational data. When you use the optional Cloud Workspace, Supabase receives and stores the account, device, vessel and voyage fields described above solely to provide authentication and cross-device workspace transfer. Supabase does not receive TrimCargo source code, camera photos, manually exported files or PDF reports through this feature. Google Play or Apple processes Pro purchase transactions under its own terms and privacy policy. The App receives only the product identifier, purchase state and transaction token needed to grant, acknowledge and restore Pro access; it does not receive payment-card details.

9. Data retention and deletion

Operational data is retained on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. Operating-system backup/device-transfer may restore local app data and entitlement preferences when enabled for your Google or Apple account. You can delete data at any time:

  • Delete an individual ship, voyage, survey or draft inside the App.
  • Use Settings → Master data to reset master records.
  • Uninstall the App — the operating system removes its private local storage, subject to backups you control.
  • Use Delete from cloud to permanently remove one selected cloud voyage and its cloud revision history. Local copies and the separately stored cloud vessel document are kept.
  • Use Delete account in Account & Cloud to permanently remove the account login and all of its cloud vessels, voyages, revisions and device registrations. The App asks for the current password again before deletion.

Cloud records remain until you delete the selected record or account. Deletion removes the data from the active cloud database immediately. Residual copies may remain temporarily in encrypted service backups until the provider's normal backup rotation completes, or where limited retention is required for security, fraud prevention or law. Deleting a cloud account does not delete local copies on your devices. If you cannot use the App, follow the instructions on our account deletion page or email alamdar_ali@outlook.com from the account email address.

10. Security

The App relies on the operating system's application sandbox to isolate stored data from other apps and uses AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption for portable voyage archives. Optional biometric lock gates sensitive ship-management operations. On Windows, the cloud refresh token is encrypted for the current Windows user with Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). On Android, the session is encrypted using a non-exportable Android Keystore key. Cloud traffic uses HTTPS, the public client key grants no owner-wide access, and Supabase row-level security checks the signed-in user for every cloud record. No security model is perfect; please keep your device locked, your operating system up to date, and install apps only from trusted distribution sources.

11. Children's privacy

TrimCargo is a professional tool intended for adult mariners (age 18 or above) and is rated accordingly in its store listings. The App does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Your rights

You can inspect and change local operational records in the Apps and export them in the supported portable formats. For an optional cloud account, you can delete it in the App and may request access, correction or export by contacting us from the account email address. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location.

13. Changes to this Policy

This Policy covers the optional account and manually controlled Cloud Workspace on Android, iOS and Windows. Store privacy declarations are reviewed whenever those features or collected data categories change.

14. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: alamdar_ali@outlook.com
