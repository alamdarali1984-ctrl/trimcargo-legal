TrimCargo customer support

For technical, cloud-account, privacy or purchase support, email alamdar_ali@outlook.com.

What to include

  • Your platform: Android, iOS or Windows.
  • The TrimCargo version shown in the App.
  • A short description of what happened and the steps that led to it.
  • The exact error message, if one appeared.

Please protect operational and account data. Do not send your password, password-recovery link, authentication token, full vessel dataset or other confidential information. We will ask for the minimum additional detail if it is needed.

Cloud account deletion

Use the in-app Delete account control or follow the account-deletion instructions.

Store purchases

Payment details, receipts, refunds and regional pricing are handled by the store where the purchase was made. TrimCargo never receives your card details.
