Delete a TrimCargo Cloud account

TrimCargo Cloud is optional. Local vessel, voyage, survey, calculation and report records do not require an account.

Delete in the App

  1. Open Account & Cloud in TrimCargo Android, iOS or Windows.
  2. Sign in to the account.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Read the warning, enter the current password, and confirm Delete account permanently.

This cannot be undone. It permanently removes the account login, cloud vessels, cloud voyages, revision history and device registrations. Local copies on your devices are kept and become independent.

If you cannot access the App

Email alamdar_ali@outlook.com from the TrimCargo Cloud account email address and request permanent account deletion. We will verify the request before deletion.

Retention

Deletion removes the data from the active cloud database. Residual copies may remain temporarily in encrypted service backups until normal backup rotation completes, or where limited retention is required for security, fraud prevention or law. Local device records and user-controlled exports are outside the cloud account and are not deleted by this request.
